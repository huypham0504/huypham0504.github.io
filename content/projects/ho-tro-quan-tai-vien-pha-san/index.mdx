## Overview

- **Role:** Assistant to an Insolvency Administrator
- **Organisation:** Hoan Cau Viet Nam Group Asset Management and Liquidation Partnership
- **Period:** 06/2025 – 11/2026
- **Scope:** Multiple corporate restructuring and bankruptcy cases across different industries

## Selected cases

Worked as an assistant to the insolvency administrator on bankruptcy cases in which my organisation was appointed by the court to manage and liquidate assets:

| Company | Basis |
|---|---|
| Do Gia Seafood Import-Export Co., Ltd. | Decision to open bankruptcy proceedings No. 31/2026/QĐST-PS dated 8 June 2026 of the People's Court of Region 1 – Ho Chi Minh City |
| Diamond Star Consulting Services JSC | Decision to open bankruptcy proceedings of the competent court |
| Duong Minh Chau Cassava Starch Co., Ltd. | Decision to open bankruptcy proceedings of the competent court |

Decisions to open bankruptcy proceedings are published on the Supreme People's Court's [national portal on restructuring and bankruptcy](https://www.toaan.gov.vn/webcenter/portal/tatc/thong-bao-tuyen-bo-pha-san).

*Case files, creditor lists and case figures are kept confidential.*

## Work

- Advised parties on corporate restructuring and bankruptcy procedures.
- Received creditors' claims, checked supporting documents and helped verify debts.
- Helped prepare lists of creditors and debtors.
- Supported the insolvency administrator in preparing and running creditors' meetings.
- Worked directly with creditors: explaining the procedure and guiding them on missing documents.
- Researched case files and compiled reports for the insolvency administrator.

## What I learned

- A solid grasp of restructuring and bankruptcy procedure under the 2025 Law on Restructuring and Bankruptcy.
- Handling large files with many creditors, where figures and deadlines must be exact.
- Communicating with creditors, debtors and the authorities running the proceedings.
