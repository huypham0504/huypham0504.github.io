## What I maintain

I adapted this portfolio to document my work, publish legal articles and learn how to maintain a bilingual website. I edit the Vietnamese and English MDX entries, add tables, diagrams and checklists to articles, and update the online CV and its downloadable PDF.

## How the site works

Projects and articles have two source files, `index.mdx` for English and `index.vi.mdx` for Vietnamese. Velite processes the content at build time. Next.js exports a static site to `out`, and [GitHub Actions](https://github.com/huypham0504/huypham0504.github.io/blob/main/.github/workflows/nextjs.yml) deploys it to GitHub Pages when changes land on `main`.
